Fraction Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out fractions, mixed numbers and decimals exactly from the whole numbers you type. It does not know how a class, recipe or specification wants an answer rounded or written. Check that where it applies.

The op truth

Two fractions and one operation, reduced. Whole numbers only, denominators at least 1.

The mixed truth

A whole number plus a fraction, as one fraction.

The decimal truth

A decimal written out as the exact fraction it names. Up to 12 digits on each side of the point.
